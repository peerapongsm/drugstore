Where Each Side Wins

tPHARM Advantages

  1. Offline-first — PouchDB works when internet drops. Critical for Thai pharmacies with unstable connections.
  2. Hardware integration — Cash drawer, credit card terminal, customer display screen.
  3. Broader product scope — Sells beauty, food, supplements, and equipment. More revenue per store.
  4. 57mm + 80mm printer — Wider hardware compatibility.
  5. Customer display screen — Professional POS counter experience.

DuckDuckDrug Advantages

  1. Full pharma compliance — Rx, dispensing log, destruction, GPP. tPHARM is retail POS with drug support bolted on.
  2. Mobile-first — Works on any phone/tablet, no installation. Check inventory floor-side.
  3. Drug label printing — Patient name, dosage, frequency, meal timing, pharmacist name per drug.
  4. Lot/batch management + FIFO — Per-batch expiry tracking, deducts oldest stock first.
  5. Purchasing workflow — PO → GR → Purchase Returns with supplier management.
  6. Drug sub-units — Sell individual tablets vs. full blister pack.
  7. Customer loyalty tiers — Auto-discount 3/5% for loyal patients.
  8. Multi-tenant SaaS — 100 stores on one platform, root admin impersonation.
  9. Camera barcode scan — Phone camera as scanner; no USB hardware needed.
  10. Deeper reports — GP%, purchase returns, payment type split, unlimited date range.
  11. Zero-touch updates — Push to Vercel = all stores get update instantly.
