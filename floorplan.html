CONTRACTOR SPECIFICATION NOTES
1. STRUCTURAL / PARTITION WORKS
  • Consultation zone: gypsum board 12 mm on 75 mm metal stud, floor-to-ceiling; confirm ceiling height before ordering studs; min 2.7 m clear height required
  • No structural demolition expected — existing shell unit assumed; confirm with building management
  • Maximum shelf load: confirm slab load capacity before wall-anchoring heavy shelving; use rawl bolts into concrete, not drywall anchors
  • Building management approval required before any drilling into structural walls or slab
  • Entry door on right (outside) wall — verify structural opening; aluminium frame glass door 900 mm × 2100 mm; single or double leaf per owner decision
2. ELECTRICAL WORKS
  • AC-1 (left wall, Zone 1–2 area): dedicated 20 A / 220 V circuit from DB
  • AC-2 (left wall, Zone 3–5 area): dedicated 20 A / 220 V circuit from DB
  • Pharma fridge (Zone 7): dedicated 15 A / 220 V circuit, UPS-backed; separate MCB labelled "FRIDGE — DO NOT SWITCH OFF"
  • Dispensing counter: 4× 15 A outlets + 1× Cat6 RJ45
  • Office: 4× 15 A outlets + 1× Cat6 RJ45
  • Consultation: 2× 15 A outlets
  • Staff zone: 2× 15 A outlets
  • Estimated total load: 2 × AC (4,400 W) + fridge (200 W) + lighting (400 W) + equipment (800 W) = ~5,800 W → minimum 32 A main MCB recommended; verify with licensed electrician
  • CCTV: 4× cameras on left wall; run Cat6 from each camera position to DVR/NVR location (suggest: Zone 6 office, back-left corner); DVR requires 2× outlets + Cat6 to network switch; power all cameras from DVR PSU or PoE switch
  • Network switch location: Z6 office — 1× wall-mount patch panel or small rack; run Cat6 home runs to counter, office, CCTV positions
3. PLUMBING WORKS
  • 2 sinks required: (1) Dispensing counter — hand-wash, mixer tap, P-trap waste; (2) Staff/utilities zone (Z8) — same spec
  • Confirm existing floor waste points before finalising counter and sink positions; reposition of waste branch may incur additional cost
  • Hot water: not required — cold-water mixer tap sufficient
  • Supply line: 15 mm copper or CPVC; waste line: 50 mm PVC to floor drain
  • Allow for waterproofing around sink bases; seal to wall with silicone
4. SHELVING, COUNTER & FIXTURES
  • OTC gondola shelving: 2 double-sided gondola runs, 150 cm H × 90 cm W bays, adjustable, powder-coated steel; 1 glass display cabinet 180 cm W at top of OTC zone (facing dispensing counter) for Rx/ข drugs displayed under lock
  • Drug storage shelving: wall-mounted heavy-duty adjustable metal shelving, floor-to-ceiling; min 15 cm clearance from floor; 4 runs along walls of Z3
  • Dispensing counter: full-width custom unit (approx 350 cm × 60 cm × 90 cm H) at bottom of Z2 facing OTC; laminate or HPL surface; integrated cable channel; internal shelves and drawers for pharmacist use; integrate sink cutout per plan
  • Locked Rx cabinet: heavy-gauge steel, 2-point lock, wall-anchored; min internal dimensions 50×35×100 cm (adjustable shelves inside)
  • Consultation partition: full-height gypsum; single-swing 500 mm door; lever handle; paint to match walls
5. FLOORING, WALLS & FINISHES
  • Sales floor (Z1–Z2): commercial vinyl plank 2 mm wear layer, or 30×30 cm ceramic tile — non-slip coefficient R9 or better, easy to clean
  • Storage and back areas (Z3–Z8): sealed epoxy coating or matching vinyl — no grout lines (prevents contamination)
  • Walls: white semi-gloss latex paint (scrubbable); tile splash-back 60 cm height at all sinks
  • Ceiling: white painted concrete or white acoustic tile; min 2.7 m clear height; LED panel lights (1 panel per zone, ~300–500 lux at work surfaces)
  • Door (main entry): aluminium frame glass door 900 mm × 2100 mm on right outside wall; push/pull bars; owner to confirm single or double leaf
6. PRE-HANDOVER CHECKLIST — FDA SITE INSPECTION READINESS
  1. All 8 zones physically demarcated and labelled as per this plan
  2. Dispensing counter hand-wash sink functional (running water, waste drain confirmed)
  3. Locked steel cabinet installed, wall-anchored, and key tested
  4. CCTV system installed — CAM-3 must have clear coverage of locked cabinet; DVR recording confirmed
  5. Pharmaceutical fridge on dedicated circuit; temperature displays 2–8 °C; temp log clipboard in place
  6. Thermo-hygrometer installed in Zone 3; reading ≤ 25 °C and ≤ 60 % RH confirmed
  7. Consultation zone partition and door installed; acoustic privacy adequate
  8. 2 × AC units operational (left wall); storage zone temperature confirmed ≤ 25 °C
  9. Drug classification signage (ก/ข/ค/ง) installed on shelving
  10. Pharmacist licence frame installed at dispensing counter at eye level
  11. Fire extinguisher bracket mounted at 120 cm; owner to hang charged extinguisher
  12. Lighting ≥ 300 lux at dispensing counter and storage areas (confirm with lux meter)
  13. All flooring non-slip, non-porous, and cleanable
  14. All electrical circuits labelled at DB; RCD/ELCB protection confirmed
  15. Snag list resolved; site clean and ready for inspector walk-through
