CONTRACTOR SPECIFICATION NOTES
1. STRUCTURAL / PARTITION WORKS
  • Consultation zone right boundary: full-height gypsum board 12 mm on 75 mm metal stud OR heavy-duty privacy screen (floor-to-ceiling); confirm ceiling height before ordering studs; min 2.7 m clear height required
  • Rest Room: full wet-area waterproofing (floor + 500 mm upstand all walls); tile finish; exhaust fan ducted to external
  • No structural demolition expected — existing shell unit assumed; confirm with building management before any wall drilling
  • Maximum shelf load: confirm slab load capacity; use rawl bolts into concrete, not drywall anchors
2. ELECTRICAL WORKS
  • AC-1 (left wall, dispensing zone height): dedicated 20 A / 220 V circuit; covers Consultation+Dispensing zone
  • AC-2 (left wall, OTC zone height): dedicated 20 A / 220 V circuit; covers OTC+Shelf + Activity zones
  • Pharma fridge: dedicated 15 A / 220 V circuit, UPS-backed; MCB labelled "FRIDGE — DO NOT SWITCH OFF"
  • Dispensing counter: 4× 15 A outlets + 1× Cat6 RJ45
  • Activity zone: 2× 15 A outlets + ceiling light
  • Rest Room: 1× 15 A shaver outlet; exhaust fan on separate switch
  • CCTV: 3× cameras on left wall; Cat6 home runs to DVR (back corner of Consultation+Dispensing zone)
  • Estimated load: 2×AC (4,400 W) + fridge (200 W) + lighting (400 W) + equipment (800 W) ≈ 5,800 W → minimum 32 A main MCB
3. PLUMBING WORKS
  • 2 sinks: (1) Dispensing counter — hand-wash, mixer tap, P-trap; (2) Rest Room — wall-hung, mixer tap, P-trap
  • Rest Room: WC pan + cistern, 100 mm soil waste; cold water supply; floor drain with waterproofing
  • Confirm existing floor waste points before finalising positions
  • Supply: 15 mm copper or CPVC; waste: 50 mm PVC (sink) / 100 mm PVC (WC)
4. SHELVING, COUNTER & FIXTURES
  • OTC+Shelf zone: 4 wall-shelf rows (right wall, heavy-duty adjustable metal, min 15 cm floor clearance, labelled by category); 3 gondola runs (150 cm H, double-sided, 90 cm W bays); glass display cabinet 162 cm W at front
  • Activity zone: 3 wall-shelf rows; fold-flat table for events; 2 chairs; maintain ≥ 2 m² clear floor
  • Dispensing counter: custom unit 165 cm × 60 cm × 90 cm H; HPL surface; cable channel; sink cutout; internal shelves + drawers
  • Locked Rx cabinet: heavy-gauge steel, 2-point lock, wall-anchored in Consultation zone; min 50×35×100 cm
  • Pharma fridge: 300 L dual-zone; 10 cm clearance all sides; dedicated 15 A MCB + UPS
5. FLOORING, WALLS & FINISHES
  • Dispensing + OTC areas: commercial vinyl plank 2 mm wear layer, or 30×30 cm ceramic tile — non-slip R9+, easy to clean
  • Storage + consultation + activity: sealed epoxy coating or matching vinyl — no grout lines
  • Rest Room: ceramic tile throughout; floor drain; waterproofing membrane under tiles
  • Walls: white semi-gloss latex (scrubbable); tile splash-back 60 cm at dispensing sink
  • Ceiling: white painted or acoustic tile; min 2.7 m clear height; LED panels 300–500 lux at all work surfaces
6. PRE-HANDOVER CHECKLIST — FDA SITE INSPECTION READINESS
  1. All 4 zones physically demarcated and labelled as per this plan
  2. Dispensing counter hand-wash sink functional
  3. Locked steel cabinet installed, wall-anchored, key tested
  4. CCTV — CAM-1 must have clear coverage of locked cabinet; DVR recording confirmed
  5. Pharmaceutical fridge on dedicated circuit; temperature 2–8 °C; temp log clipboard in place
  6. Thermo-hygrometer installed in OTC+Shelf zone; ≤ 25 °C and ≤ 60 % RH confirmed
  7. Consultation area privacy screen installed; acoustic privacy adequate
  8. 2 × AC units operational; storage zone ≤ 25 °C confirmed
  9. Drug classification signage ก/ข/ค/ง installed on shelving
  10. Pharmacist licence frame at dispensing counter at eye level
  11. Rest Room functional (WC, sink, exhaust fan, door lock)
  12. Lighting ≥ 300 lux at dispensing counter and storage areas
  13. All flooring non-slip, non-porous, cleanable
  14. All electrical circuits labelled at DB; RCD/ELCB protection confirmed
  15. Snag list resolved; site clean and ready for inspector walk-through
